To solve this problem, we need to use the Doppler effect formula for sound waves. The formula is:

f' = f_0 \* (v + v_s) / (v - v_s)

where:

* f' is the observed frequency
* f_0 is the emitted frequency (440 Hz)
* v is the speed of sound (343 m/s)
* v_s is the speed of the source (ambulance) (30 m/s)

First, we need to plug in the values into the formula:

f' = 440 \* (343 + 30) / (343 - 30)
f' = 440 \* 373 / 313
f' = 440 \* 1.19
f' = 524.6 Hz

So, the person hears a frequency of approximately 524.6 Hz.
