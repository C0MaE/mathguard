To solve this problem, we need to use the equation for the period of small oscillations of a pendulum, which is given by:

T = 2π √(L/g)

where T is the period of small oscillations, L is the length of the pendulum, and g is the acceleration due to gravity.

Given that L = 1 m and g = 9.81 m/s^2, we can plug these values into the equation:

T = 2π √(1/9.81)
T = 2π √(0.101)
