To solve this problem, we can use the Carnot efficiency formula, which is given by:

η = 1 - (T_c / T_h)

where η is the efficiency, T_c is the temperature of the cold reservoir, and T_h is the temperature of the hot reservoir.

First, we need to find the efficiency of the Carnot engine:

η = 1 - (300 K / 600 K)
= 1 - 0.5
= 0.5

Next, we can use the efficiency to find the work delivered per cycle:

W = η \* Q_h
= 0.5 \* 1000 J
= 500 J

Therefore, the work delivered per cycle is 500 J.
