To solve this problem, we need to use the formula for the time constant of a capacitor discharging through a resistor:

τ = RC

where τ is the time constant, R is the resistance, and C is the capacitance.

Given values:
R = 10 kΩ = 10,000 Ω
C = 47 μF = 0.000047 F

Now, we can calculate the time constant:

τ = RC
= (10,000 Ω) × (0.000047 F)
= 0.47 s

The time it takes for the voltage to drop to half its initial value is given by:

t = τ ln(2)

where ln(2) is the natural logarithm of 2.

Now, we can calculate the time:

t = τ ln(2)
= 0.47 s × ln(2)
= 0.47 s × 0.693
= 0.325 s

Therefore, the time it takes for the voltage to drop to half its initial value is:

RESULT
t [time until half the initial voltage] = 0.325 s
