To determine the magnitude of the acceleration of the blocks and the tension in the rope, we analyze the system of two masses connected by a massless rope over a massless, frictionless pulley. The key is to apply Newton’s second law to each block and solve the resulting equations simultaneously.

---

### Step 1: Define the forces and acceleration

Let:
- $ m_1 = 3 \, \mathrm{kg} $ (heavier block)
- $ m_2 = 2 \, \mathrm{kg} $ (lighter block)
- $ g = 9.81 \, \mathrm{m/s^2} $
- $ a $: acceleration of the blocks (same magnitude for both)
- $ T $: tension in the rope

Assume the heavier block $ m_1 $ accelerates downward, and the lighter block $ m_2 $ accelerates upward.

---

### Step 2: Apply Newton’s second law

For block $ m_1 $:
$$
m_1 g - T = m_1 a \tag{1}
$$

For block $ m_2 $:
$$
T - m_2 g = m_2 a \tag{2}
$$

---

### Step 3: Solve for acceleration $ a $

Add equations (1) and (2) to eliminate $ T $:
$$
m_1 g - m_2 g = m_1 a + m_2 a
$$
$$
g (m_1 - m_2) = a (m_1 + m_2)
$$
$$
a = \frac{g (m_1 - m_2)}{m_1 + m_2}
$$

Substitute the values:
$$
a = \frac{9.81 \cdot (3 - 2)}{3 + 2} = \frac{9.81}{5} = 1.962 \, \mathrm{m/s^2}
$$

---

### Step 4: Solve for tension $ T $

Use equation (2):
$$
T = m_2 (g + a)
$$
$$
T = 2 \cdot (9.81 + 1.962) = 2 \cdot 11.772 = 23.544 \, \mathrm{N}
$$

Alternatively, using equation (1):
$$
T = m_1 (g - a) = 3 \cdot (9.81 - 1.962) = 3 \cdot 7.848 = 23.544 \, \mathrm{N}
$$

Both methods yield the same result.

---

### Final Answer

RESULT  
a [magnitude of the acceleration] = $\frac{g(m_1 - m_2)}{m_1 + m_2}$ = 1.962 m/s²  
F [rope tension] = $\frac{2m_1m_2g}{m_1 + m_2}$ = 23.544 N
