To determine the image distance $ b $ (denoted as $ d_i $) for a thin converging lens, we use the **lens formula**:

$$
\frac{1}{f} = \frac{1}{a} + \frac{1}{b}
$$

Where:
- $ f = 10\,\mathrm{cm} $ is the focal length,
- $ a = 15\,\mathrm{cm} $ is the object distance,
- $ b $ is the image distance (to be solved).

---

### Step 1: Rearranging the formula to solve for $ b $

$$
\frac{1}{b} = \frac{1}{f} - \frac{1}{a}
$$

---

### Step 2: Substituting the known values

$$
\frac{1}{b} = \frac{1}{10} - \frac{1}{15}
$$

Find a common denominator (LCM of 10 and 15 is 30):

$$
\frac{1}{b} = \frac{3}{30} - \frac{2}{30} = \frac{1}{30}
$$

---

### Step 3: Solving for $ b $

$$
b = 30\,\mathrm{cm}
$$

---

### Final Answer

RESULT  
b [image distance] = $\frac{1}{\frac{1}{f} - \frac{1}{a}}$ = $30$ cm
