To determine the final temperature of air after an adiabatic and reversible compression, we use the thermodynamic relation for an ideal gas undergoing an adiabatic process:

$$
T_1 V_1^{\gamma - 1} = T_2 V_2^{\gamma - 1}
$$

Rearranging to solve for $ T_2 $:

$$
T_2 = T_1 \left( \frac{V_1}{V_2} \right)^{\gamma - 1}
$$

---

### **Given:**
- Initial temperature: $ T_1 = 300\,\mathrm{K} $
- Initial volume: $ V_1 = 1\,\mathrm{L} $
- Final volume: $ V_2 = 0.1\,\mathrm{L} $
- Specific heat ratio: $ \gamma = 1.4 $

---

### **Step-by-step Calculation:**

1. **Compute the volume ratio:**
   $$
   \frac{V_1}{V_2} = \frac{1}{0.1} = 10
   $$

2. **Compute the exponent:**
   $$
   \gamma - 1 = 1.4 - 1 = 0.4
   $$

3. **Apply the formula:**
   $$
   T_2 = 300 \times 10^{0.4}
   $$

4. **Evaluate the exponent:**
   $$
   10^{0.4} \approx 2.511886
   $$

5. **Final temperature:**
   $$
   T_2 \approx 300 \times 2.511886 = 753.5658\,\mathrm{K}
   $$

---

### **Final Answer:**

RESULT  
T2 [final temperature] = $ T_1 \left( \frac{V_1}{V_2} \right)^{\gamma - 1} $ = 753.6 K
