To determine the volume flow rate $ Q_v $ of water exiting the hole in the tank, we apply **Torricelli's Law**, which is derived from Bernoulli's principle. This law gives the velocity of fluid exiting an orifice as:

$$
v = \sqrt{2gh}
$$

Where:
- $ g = 9.81 \, \mathrm{m/s^2} $ is the acceleration due to gravity,
- $ h = 2 \, \mathrm{m} $ is the height of the water above the hole.

---

### Step 1: Calculate the velocity of the fluid exiting the hole

$$
v = \sqrt{2gh} = \sqrt{2 \cdot 9.81 \cdot 2} = \sqrt{39.24} \approx 6.264 \, \mathrm{m/s}
$$

---

### Step 2: Convert the area of the hole to SI units

The area of the hole is given as $ A = 1 \, \mathrm{cm^2} $. Converting to square meters:

$$
A = 1 \, \mathrm{cm^2} = (1 \times 10^{-2} \, \mathrm{m})^2 = 1 \times 10^{-4} \, \mathrm{m^2}
$$

---

### Step 3: Compute the volume flow rate

$$
Q_v = A \cdot v = (1 \times 10^{-4} \, \mathrm{m^2}) \cdot (6.264 \, \mathrm{m/s}) = 6.264 \times 10^{-4} \, \mathrm{m^3/s}
$$

Convert to liters per second (since $ 1 \, \mathrm{m^3} = 1000 \, \mathrm{L} $):

$$
Q_v = 6.264 \times 10^{-4} \times 1000 = 0.6264 \, \mathrm{L/s}
$$

---

### Final Answer

RESULT  
Qv [volume flow rate] = $ A \cdot \sqrt{2gh} $ = $ 0.626 \, \mathrm{L/s} $
