To determine the elapsed time $ t $ after which a quarter (i.e., $ p = 0.25 $) of the original radioactive nuclei remain, we use the exponential decay formula based on the half-life:

$$
N(t) = N_0 \left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^{t / T_{1/2}}
$$

We are given that $ N(t)/N_0 = 0.25 $, so:

$$
0.25 = \left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^{t / T_{1/2}}
$$

Recognizing that $ 0.25 = \left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^2 $, we equate the exponents:

$$
\frac{t}{T_{1/2}} = 2
$$

Solving for $ t $:

$$
t = 2 \cdot T_{1/2}
$$

Substituting the given half-life $ T_{1/2} = 5730 $ years:

$$
t = 2 \cdot 5730 = 11460
$$
