Step 1: Isothermal reversible expansion of an ideal gas. The internal energy is unchanged, so the work equals the heat absorbed:
W = integral from V1 to V2 of p dV = integral of n R T / V dV = n R T ln(V2 / V1).

Step 2: Numbers.
n R T = 2 mol * 8.314 J/(mol K) * 300 K = 4988.4 J.
V2 / V1 = 30 L / 10 L = 3, ln(3) = 1.0986.
W = 4988.4 J * 1.0986 = 5480 J.

RESULT
W [work done by the gas] = n R T ln(V2 / V1) = 5.48e3 J
