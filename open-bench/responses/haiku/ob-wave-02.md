Step 1: Doppler effect with a moving source and a stationary observer. The source approaches, so the wavelength in front is compressed:
f = f0 * v / (v - v_s).

Step 2: Numbers, with f0 = 440 Hz, v = 343 m/s, v_s = 30 m/s.
v - v_s = 313 m/s.
f = 440 Hz * 343 / 313 = 440 Hz * 1.0958 = 482 Hz.

RESULT
f [observed frequency] = f0 * c / (c - v) = 482 Hz
