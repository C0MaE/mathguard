Step 1: Energy stored in the compressed spring.
E_spring = (1/2) k d^2 = 0.5 * 200 N/m * (0.10 m)^2 = 0.5 * 200 * 0.01 J = 1.0 J.

Step 2: All of this energy goes into the ball as it leaves the spring (the change of height during the spring's relaxation is neglected). At the highest point the ball's kinetic energy is zero, so the potential energy gained equals the spring energy:
m g h = (1/2) k d^2  =>  h = k d^2 / (2 m g).

Step 3: Numbers, with m = 0.050 kg.
m g = 0.050 * 9.81 = 0.4905 N.
h = 1.0 J / 0.4905 N = 2.04 m.

RESULT
h [maximum height] = k d^2 / (2 m g) = 2.04 m
