Step 1: Torricelli's law. Bernoulli's equation between the free surface (speed ~0, pressure atmospheric) and the hole (pressure atmospheric), with the tank much wider than the hole so the surface speed is negligible:
v = sqrt(2 g H) = sqrt(2 * 9.81 * 2) = sqrt(39.24) = 6.264 m/s.

Step 2: Volume flow rate.
Qv = A v = 1.0e-4 m^2 * 6.264 m/s = 6.264e-4 m^3/s.

Step 3: Convert to litres per second (1 m^3 = 1000 L).
Qv = 6.264e-4 * 1000 L/s = 0.626 L/s.

RESULT
Qv [volume flow rate] = A sqrt(2 g H) = 0.626 L/s
