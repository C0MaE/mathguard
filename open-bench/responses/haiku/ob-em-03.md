Step 1: Ampere's law for an infinitely long straight wire.
B(r) = mu0 * I / (2 * pi * r).

Step 2: Numbers, with r = 0.05 m and I = 10 A.
mu0 * I = 1.25664e-6 * 10 = 1.25664e-5 T m.
2 * pi * r = 2 * 3.14159 * 0.05 = 0.314159 m.
B = 1.25664e-5 / 0.314159 = 4.0e-5 T.

(Check: mu0/(2 pi) = 2e-7 N/A^2, so B = 2e-7 * 10 / 0.05 = 4e-5 T.)

RESULT
B [magnetic flux density] = mu0 * I / (2 * pi * r) = 4.0e-5 T
