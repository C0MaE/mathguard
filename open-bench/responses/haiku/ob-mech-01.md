Step 1: Time of fall for a horizontal launch from height H (no horizontal force, vertical motion free fall from rest).
H = (1/2) g t^2  =>  t = sqrt(2H/g) = sqrt(2 * 20 / 9.81) = sqrt(4.0775) = 2.0193 s.

Step 2: Horizontal distance (uniform horizontal velocity v0).
x = v0 * t = 8 m/s * 2.0193 s = 16.15 m.

RESULT
x [horizontal distance from the foot of the cliff] = v0 * sqrt(2H/g) = 16.15 m
