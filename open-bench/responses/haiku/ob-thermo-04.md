Step 1: Reversible adiabatic process for an ideal gas: T V^(gamma - 1) = constant, so
T2 = T1 (V1 / V2)^(gamma - 1).

Step 2: Numbers, with gamma = 1.4 (gamma - 1 = 0.4) and V1 / V2 = 1 L / 0.1 L = 10.
T2 = 300 K * 10^0.4 = 300 K * 2.512 = 753.6 K.

RESULT
T2 [final temperature] = T1 * (V1 / V2)^(gamma - 1) = 754 K
