Step 1: Lorentz force provides the centripetal force.
q v B = m v^2 / r  =>  r = m v / (q B).

Step 2: Numbers.
m v = 1.6726e-27 kg * 1e6 m/s = 1.6726e-21 kg m/s.
q B = 1.602e-19 C * 0.5 T = 8.01e-20 N s/m... i.e. C T.
r = 1.6726e-21 / 8.01e-20 = 0.02088 m.

So the radius is about 2.09 cm.

RESULT
rc [radius of the circle] = m * v / (q * B) = 0.0209 m
