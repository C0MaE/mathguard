Step 1: Orbital radius measured from Earth's centre.
r = R_E + h = 6371 km + 400 km = 6771 km = 6.771e6 m.

Step 2: Kepler's third law for a circular orbit (gravity = centripetal force).
G M m / r^2 = m (2 pi / T)^2 r  =>  T = 2 pi sqrt(r^3 / (G M)).

Step 3: Numbers.
r^3 = (6.771e6)^3 = 3.1042e20 m^3.
G M = 6.674e-11 * 5.972e24 = 3.9857e14 m^3/s^2.
r^3 / (G M) = 3.1042e20 / 3.9857e14 = 7.788e5 s^2.
sqrt(7.788e5) = 882.5 s.
T = 2 pi * 882.5 s = 5545 s, which is about 92 min (1.54 h).

RESULT
T [orbital period] = 2 pi sqrt((R_E + h)^3 / (G M_E)) = 5.55e3 s
