Step 1: Capacitance of a parallel-plate capacitor in vacuum.
C = eps0 * A / d = 8.854e-12 F/m * 0.01 m^2 / 0.001 m = 8.854e-11 F.

Step 2: Energy stored in a charged capacitor.
W = (1/2) C U^2 = 0.5 * 8.854e-11 F * (100 V)^2 = 0.5 * 8.854e-11 * 1e4 J = 4.427e-7 J.

Equivalently, W = (1/2) eps0 A U^2 / d, which gives the same value.

RESULT
Wel [stored energy] = (1/2) * eps0 * A * U^2 / d = 4.43e-7 J
