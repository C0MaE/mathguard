Step 1: Discharge law of an RC circuit.
U(t) = U0 * exp(-t / (R C)).

Step 2: Condition for half the initial voltage.
U(t)/U0 = 1/2  =>  exp(-t / (R C)) = 1/2  =>  t = R C ln 2.

Step 3: Numbers.
R C = 1e4 Ohm * 47e-6 F = 0.47 s.
t = 0.47 s * 0.6931 = 0.3258 s.

RESULT
t [time until half the initial voltage] = R * C * ln(2) = 0.326 s
