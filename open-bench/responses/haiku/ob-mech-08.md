Step 1: Tsiolkovsky rocket equation (no external forces, exhaust speed u relative to the rocket).
m dv = -u dm  =>  dv = u * ln(m0 / m1) for the burn from m0 to m1.

Step 2: Numbers.
m0 / m1 = 1000 / 400 = 2.5.
ln(2.5) = 0.9163.
dv = 3000 m/s * 0.9163 = 2749 m/s.

RESULT
dv [gain in speed] = u * ln(m0 / m1) = 2.75e3 m/s
