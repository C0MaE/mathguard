Step 1: Energy balance in the insulated vessel (negligible heat capacity of the vessel). The heat lost by the hot water equals the heat gained by the cold water. Both are water, so the specific heat c cancels:
m1 c (T1 - Tf) = m2 c (Tf - T2)  =>  Tf = (m1 T1 + m2 T2) / (m1 + m2).

Step 2: Numbers.
m1 T1 = 0.2 * 353.15 = 70.63 kg K.
m2 T2 = 0.3 * 293.15 = 87.945 kg K.
Sum = 158.575 kg K; m1 + m2 = 0.5 kg.
Tf = 158.575 / 0.5 = 317.15 K (about 44 deg C).

RESULT
Tf [final temperature] = (m1 T1 + m2 T2) / (m1 + m2) = 317.15 K
