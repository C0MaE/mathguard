Step 1: Perfectly inelastic collision (bullet stays in the block). Momentum is conserved.
m v = (m + M) V  =>  V = m v / (m + M) = 0.010 kg * 400 m/s / 2.010 kg = 1.990 m/s.

Step 2: The block with the embedded bullet swings up. Energy is conserved after the collision (string tension does no work):
(1/2)(m + M) V^2 = (m + M) g h  =>  h = V^2 / (2 g).

Step 3: Numbers.
V^2 = 1.990^2 = 3.960 m^2/s^2.
h = 3.960 / (2 * 9.81) = 3.960 / 19.62 = 0.2019 m.

RESULT
h [rise of the block] = (m v / (m + M))^2 / (2 g) = 0.202 m
