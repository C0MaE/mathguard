Step 1: Thin-lens equation (real-is-positive convention).
1/f = 1/a + 1/b  =>  1/b = 1/f - 1/a.

Step 2: Numbers, with f = 10 cm and a = 15 cm.
1/b = 1/10 - 1/15 = (3 - 2) / 30 = 1/30 cm^-1.
b = 30 cm.

The object is outside the focal length, so the image is real, inverted and located 30 cm behind the lens (magnification -2).

RESULT
b [image distance] = 1 / (1/f - 1/a) = 30 cm
