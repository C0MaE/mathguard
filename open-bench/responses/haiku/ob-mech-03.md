Step 1: Forces along and perpendicular to the plane.
Along the plane (down the slope): m g sin(alpha) - mu m g cos(alpha) = m a.
Perpendicular: N = m g cos(alpha) (no acceleration perpendicular to the plane).

Step 2: Acceleration (mass cancels).
a = g (sin(alpha) - mu cos(alpha)).

Step 3: Numbers, with alpha = 30 deg, sin = 0.5, cos = 0.8660, mu = 0.2.
sin(alpha) - mu cos(alpha) = 0.5 - 0.2 * 0.8660 = 0.5 - 0.1732 = 0.3268.
a = 9.81 * 0.3268 = 3.21 m/s^2.

RESULT
a [acceleration along the plane] = g (sin(alpha) - mu cos(alpha)) = 3.21 m/s^2
