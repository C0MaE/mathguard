Step 1: Physical pendulum. For small oscillations
T = 2 pi sqrt(I / (m g d)),
where I is the moment of inertia about the pivot and d the distance from the pivot to the centre of mass.

Step 2: Rod pivoted at one end.
I = (1/3) m L^2 (parallel-axis theorem: I_cm = m L^2/12, plus m (L/2)^2 gives m L^2/3).
d = L/2.
I / (m g d) = (m L^2/3) / (m g L/2) = 2 L / (3 g).

Step 3: Period.
T = 2 pi sqrt(2 L / (3 g)) = 2 pi sqrt(2 * 1 / (3 * 9.81)) = 2 pi sqrt(0.06796) = 2 pi * 0.2607 = 1.638 s.

RESULT
T [period of small oscillations] = 2 pi sqrt(2 L / (3 g)) = 1.64 s
