Step 1: Total internal reflection requires the angle of incidence to exceed the critical angle, given by Snell's law with refraction angle 90 deg:
n1 sin(theta_c) = n2 sin(90 deg) = n2  =>  sin(theta_c) = n2 / n1.

Step 2: Numbers.
sin(theta_c) = 1.0 / 1.5 = 0.6667.
theta_c = arcsin(0.6667) = 41.8 deg.

RESULT
theta [critical angle] = arcsin(n2 / n1) = 41.8 deg
