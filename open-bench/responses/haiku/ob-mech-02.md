Step 1: Equations of motion (take m1 = 3 kg downward, m2 = 2 kg upward, the heavier side accelerates down).
m1 g - T = m1 a
T - m2 g = m2 a

Step 2: Add the equations to eliminate T.
(m1 - m2) g = (m1 + m2) a  =>  a = (m1 - m2) g / (m1 + m2) = 1 * 9.81 / 5 = 1.962 m/s^2.

Step 3: Tension from the second equation.
T = m2 (g + a) = 2 * (9.81 + 1.962) = 2 * 11.772 = 23.54 N.

Check with the first equation: T = m1 (g - a) = 3 * (9.81 - 1.962) = 3 * 7.848 = 23.54 N.

RESULT
a [magnitude of the acceleration] = (m1 - m2) g / (m1 + m2) = 1.96 m/s^2
F [rope tension] = 2 m1 m2 g / (m1 + m2) = 23.5 N
