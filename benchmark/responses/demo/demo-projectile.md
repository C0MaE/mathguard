The vertical motion is $y(t) = v_0 \sin\alpha\, t - \tfrac12 g t^2$.
From $y(T) = 0$ the time of flight is $T = \frac{2 v_0 \sin\alpha}{g}$.

Horizontally, $x(T) = v_0 \cos\alpha \cdot T = \frac{2 v_0^2 \sin\alpha\cos\alpha}{g} = \frac{v_0^2 \sin 2\alpha}{g}$.

With numbers: $R = \frac{(20\,\mathrm{m/s})^2 \sin 60^\circ}{9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}} \approx 35.3\,\mathrm{m}$.

R = v0^2 sin(2 alpha) / g = 35.3 m
